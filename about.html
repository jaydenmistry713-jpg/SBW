Our Story

Born from a Passion for Beautiful Events

SBW Events has grown into one of Scotland's most trusted names in weddings and events. Based in Scotland and operating across the whole country, we bring the same care and attention to every occasion — from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Our team is built on a foundation of genuine care, cultural understanding, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. We know that every event tells a unique story, and we are privileged to help tell yours.

Whether it's an elegantly dressed ceremony space, the fragrance of fresh flowers, the warmth of a perfectly curated menu, or the seamless flow of an on-the-day schedule — every detail matters to us because it matters to you.

Our Journey

Six Years in the Making

From a mother-and-daughter dream to one of Scotland's most trusted event teams.

  1. Where It Began

    A Mother & Daughter, a Shared Vision

    SBW Events began as a mother-and-daughter partnership just before 2020 — family-run from the very first day. We started with smaller, bespoke celebrations, pouring care into every detail for the South Asian community that first welcomed us.

  2. Growing Together

    Built on Our Community's Trust

    The response was overwhelming. Year after year, the support of our community allowed us to grow — building a dedicated team, multiple vans, a warehouse, a showroom, and a strong support system behind every event.

  3. Today

    Bigger Celebrations, the Same Care

    Six years on, we deliver events on a scale we once only imagined — celebrations for up to 500 guests, and even multiple events in a single day, without ever losing the attention to detail that defines us.

  4. Going Further

    Destination Weddings

    In the last couple of years we've taken our work further afield, designing and delivering destination weddings for couples who wanted us by their side wherever they chose to celebrate.

  5. Always

    Forever Creating Something New

    What drives us is creativity. We love exploring fresh and challenging concepts, and we never settle for repeating the same décor — every event we create is designed entirely around the people it's for.
