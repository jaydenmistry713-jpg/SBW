Planning a Large Celebration? Catering for Big Guest Lists

Home / Blog / Catering at Scale
Catering 3 April 2026 5 min read

Feeding 300, 400 or 500 guests — without anyone waiting too long or a single dish running cold — takes far more planning than people expect. Get it right and the food becomes one of the day’s highlights.

Catering at scale is a different discipline from a small dinner. The recipes might be the same, but the logistics, timing and service all change. Here’s what we think about when we’re planning food for a large celebration.

Get your numbers genuinely accurate

At small scale a few extra guests barely register; at large scale, miscounting ripples through quantities, staffing and seating. Confirm final numbers properly, account for suppliers and children, and agree a clear cut-off date for changes with your caterer.

Choose the right service style

How the food is served matters as much as what’s on the plate, and each style has trade-offs at scale.

  • Buffet — flexible and generous, with plenty of choice, but needs enough stations and a sensible flow so queues never build.
  • Table service — elegant and calm for guests, but relies on the right staffing levels and kitchen capacity to land every course on time.

The best choice depends on your venue, your numbers and the atmosphere you want.

Plan for dietary and cultural requirements

Across a large guest list you’ll always have a range of needs — vegetarian, vegan, allergies, and dishes prepared to specific cultural or religious standards. These should be designed in from the start, not bolted on. Clear labelling on the day reassures guests and keeps service smooth.

Respect the venue’s kitchen and logistics

Not every beautiful venue has a kitchen built for hundreds of covers. Ask early about power, prep space, refrigeration and access. Knowing the constraints lets your caterer plan around them — with a field kitchen if needed — rather than discovering them on the day.

Mind the timing and the flow

Food has to fit the running order of the day, not interrupt it. Serving hundreds of people takes time, so the schedule needs to allow for it while keeping energy up. This is where catering and coordination have to work hand in hand.

We regularly cater celebrations of up to 500 guests — and even multiple events in a single day — so the scale never comes at the cost of the detail.

Whether it’s an intimate dinner or a banquet for hundreds, our catering service is built to deliver food that’s as memorable as it is beautifully presented.
