5 Reasons to Have a Coordinator on Your Wedding Day

Home / Blog / Wedding Day Coordinator
Wedding Tips 12 June 2026 5 min read

You've planned for months — maybe years. The last thing you want on the day itself is to be chasing the florist, directing guests, or answering the caterer's questions while you're meant to be getting married. That's exactly what a wedding day coordinator is for.

There's a common myth that an on-the-day coordinator is a luxury only the grandest weddings need. In our experience across hundreds of celebrations in Scotland, it's the opposite — coordination is the single thing that most reliably turns a stressful day into a joyful one, whatever the size or budget. Here are five reasons we'd recommend one to almost every couple.

1

You get to be a guest at your own wedding

When someone else is holding the schedule, you, your partner and your families are free to actually be present. No glancing at the clock, no being pulled aside with logistics questions — just the day you planned, lived rather than managed.

2

Someone owns the timeline

A wedding runs on a surprising amount of choreography — hair and makeup, ceremony, photographs, speeches, the meal, the first dance. A coordinator builds a minute-by-minute running order and quietly keeps every supplier moving to it, so the day flows instead of stalling.

3

Problems get solved before you hear about them

A buttonhole that's gone missing, a supplier stuck in traffic, a table setting that needs reworking — small hiccups happen at almost every wedding. The difference is whether they reach you. A good coordinator fixes them in the background so your memory of the day stays untroubled.

4

Your suppliers have a single point of contact

Caterer, photographer, florist, DJ, venue — on the day, each of them has questions. Without a coordinator, those questions land on you or a family member. With one, every supplier knows exactly who to ask, and everyone is working from the same plan.

5

The whole day stays calm

The hardest job to hand over is the invisible one — the worrying. When a professional is carrying the responsibility for everything running on time, that weight lifts off you and your loved ones. Calm is contagious, and it shows in your photographs and in how the day feels.

Coordination versus full planning — what's the difference?

Full planning supports you across the whole journey: sourcing and briefing suppliers, managing budgets and timelines, and shaping the design of your day from the very start. On-the-day coordination is a lighter-touch service for couples who've done much of the planning themselves but want a professional to take the reins for the final stretch and the day itself.

There's no single right answer — it depends on how much you want to hand over. If you're not sure which suits you, that's exactly the kind of thing we're happy to talk through, with no obligation.

The best compliment we receive is when a couple tells us they didn't have to think about a single thing on their wedding day. That's the whole point.

At SBW Events we offer both event planning and co-ordination and on-the-day event management for weddings and celebrations across Scotland. Whatever stage you're at, we'd love to help you enjoy your day to the full.
